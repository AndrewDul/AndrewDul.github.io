About me

I like understanding how things work — then making them work better.

I’m a First Class Computer Software Engineering graduate and an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at De Montfort University. I build across web, Android, data and AI projects, and I enjoy the point where an idea becomes a working system.

My way of working is practical and curious. I break a problem into smaller parts, test the important details, and keep looking until I understand the real cause — not just a quick workaround. I learn best by building, checking the result and improving it step by step.

Outside code, I’m interested in nature, mathematics, physics, astronomy and robotics. I like asking how the world works, from patterns inside a system to the bigger questions above us. That curiosity is a big part of why I enjoy building technology.
