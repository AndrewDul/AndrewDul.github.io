About me

I like systems that make sense when you look closely.

I’m a First Class BSc (Hons) Computer Software Engineering graduate and am studying for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at De Montfort University. My work ranges from Android and web interfaces to local AI, cameras, sensors and Raspberry Pi projects.

My approach is practical: test small parts, trace the real cause, and leave the next person a clear path through the work.

I’m also interested in mathematics, astronomy and physics. This is a side of my work and learning that I’ll develop here over time.
